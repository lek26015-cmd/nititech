## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an avant-garde editorial atmosphere calibrated for modern legal technology and intellectual capital. It intersects precision engineering with cultural distinction, catering to visionary general counsels, specialized partners, and enterprise technologists who reject generic corporate sterility.

The visual dialect merges **Neo-Brutalist Grid Rigor** with **Refined Dark Glassmorphism**. Layouts prioritize structural honesty: sharp visual frameworks, deliberate asymmetric tensions, hairline dividers, and atmospheric luminosity. Visual hierarchy is deliberate, delivering high-contrast readability against a deep chromatic void. The interface should feel like an elite investigative publication crossed with a mission-critical computational terminal—authoritative, razor-sharp, and technologically transcendent.

## Colors

The palette operates on absolute darkness punctuated by precise optical wavelengths:

- **Base Void (`#0A0B0E`):** A cold obsidian anchor creating limitless negative space and total contrast.
- **Primary Aureum (`#D4AF37`):** An architectural, desaturated imperial gold serving as the standard for editorial pedigree, key metadata highlights, and prestige accents.
- **Secondary Cyan Glow (`#00F0FF`):** An electric digital impulse used sparingly for interactive cues, dynamic status points, and speculative computational actions.
- **Tertiary Amber Spark (`#FF7A00`):** An urgent chromatic accent indicating live intelligence feeds, strict alerts, and temporal tracking.
- **Surfaces & Tonal Layers:** Structural surfaces utilize translucent obsidian values (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.7)` to `rgba(26, 30, 39, 0.85)`) framed by hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Typography & High Contrast:** Pure text values prioritize accessibility with Primary Editorial White (`#F5F6F8`), Secondary Slate (`#8E93A0`), and Dim Muted Matrix (`#4F5462`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intellectual tension between three distinct engines:

1. **Newsreader (Display & Hero Editorial):** Infuses long-form legal thought leadership, case titles, and primary headers with academic weight, high contrast, and refined literary authority. Italicized cuts are leveraged for conceptual emphasis.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Structural Interface & Body):** Delivers clean readability, ultra-sharp rendering across displays, and neutral ergonomics for system navigation, analytical metrics, and multi-clause documentation.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Data Metadata & Technical Anchors):** Enforces neo-brutalist precision across clause numbering, hash signatures, timestamping, taxonomy chips, and legal indexes. Always rendered with explicit tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a strict 12-column responsive grid framed by visible coordinates and razor-thin containment lines:

- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns, 2rem gutters, 4rem canvas margin. Asymmetrical balance is encouraged—pairing dense data rails (3 columns) with broad editorial expanses (9 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 2.5rem margin. Side rails collapse into modular analytical stacks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1rem margin. Strict vertical stacks anchored by monospaced column indices (e.g., `[COL_01]`).

Elements conform to structural division lines: sections terminate in explicit 1px horizontal delimiters (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). Whitespace is deliberate and cavernous (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) around editorial statements, contrasting with condensed metrics within glass data cells.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth abandons standard drop shadows in favor of **Luminescent Planar Layering**:

- **Substrate Layer (Level 0):** Flat `#0A0B0E` canvas, occasionally surfaced with a faint 24px technical grid in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Glassmorphic Panes (Level 1):** Background `rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.75)`, 16px backdrop-filter blur, enclosed in a 1px boundary of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active / Elevated Panes (Level 2):** Background `rgba(22, 26, 35, 0.85)`, 24px backdrop-filter blur, framed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`.
- **Volumetric Optical Glows:** Interactive focal points, active badges, or modal triggers project subtle directional radial halos rather than shadows:
  - *Aureum Aura:* `0 0 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`
  - *Cyan Vector:* `0 0 40px -6px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.18)`
  - *Crimson Alert:* `0 0 32px -4px rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.20)`

## Shapes

The shape geometry embraces an architectural, near-orthogonal discipline. Roundedness is kept tight:

- Standard structural elements, cards, and modal sheets adhere to `roundedness: 1` (4px corner radius), maintaining severe neo-brutalist precision without harsh terminal points.
- Outer glassmorphic containers and system dialogs scale to `rounded-lg` (8px), preventing visual fatigue on expansive layouts.
- Buttons and interactive inputs maintain rigid 4px bounds or strict 0px chamfered corners for technical controls. Pills and organic continuous curvature are strictly forbidden, reinforcing mechanical and procedural discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Editorial:** Crisp obsidian core with solid Gold (`#D4AF37`) fill, text in `#0A0B0E` (Plus Jakarta Sans, SemiBold, 13px). Hover activates a subtle exterior aureum bloom (`0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Terminal:** Frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text in `#F5F6F8`. On hover, the border transitions to Cyan (`#00F0FF`) with a faint terminal inner glow.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text with JetBrains Mono indexing (e.g., `→ EXECUTE_CLAUSE`), flanked by an animated underline that expands on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Micro-proportions with `label-caps` typography (10px uppercase, JetBrains Mono).
- Enclosed in a 1px border with dark semi-transparent fill (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`).
- Status-driven color pulses: Live legal analysis tags feature a 4px flashing indicator dot (Cyan `#00F0FF` or Amber `#FF7A00`).

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Glassmorphic architecture: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, fill `rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.72)`.
- Enclosed with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Cards incorporate top-corner index tokens in `JetBrains Mono` (e.g., `SEC // 04.A`) in dim slate (`#4F5462`), creating technical authenticity.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `rgba(10, 11, 14, 0.8)`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus state activates a sharp 1px border in Cyan (`#00F0FF`) accompanied by an ambient cyan rim glow; no focus ring outlines.
- Placeholder text set in Plus Jakarta Sans (`#4F5462`), transitioning to high-contrast `#F5F6F8` upon text entry.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Controls
- Square 16px geometry with 2px radius. Border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Checked state fills with Solid Gold (`#D4AF37`) bearing an obsidian checkmark icon.
- Toggles utilize rectangular structural tracks with linear sliding pips, avoiding rounded pills.

### Specialized Legal Tech Modules
- **Clause Redline Matrix:** Dual-column split comparison card with deleted text highlighted in muted crimson glass and proposed modifications encapsulated in low-opacity cyan strokes.
- **Telemetry Rail:** Vertical monospaced timestamp thread documenting cryptographically verified smart contract events, utilizing amber glowing line nodes.
